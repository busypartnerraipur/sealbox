Authors

Mounir IDRASSI (AM Crypto, https://www.linkedin.com/in/idrassi) is the creator and main developer of Sealbox. He managed all development and deployment aspects on all supported platforms (Windows,Linux and OSX).

Alex Kolotnikov (https://ru.linkedin.com/in/alex-kolotnikov-6625568b) is the author of Sealbox EFI bootloader. He manages all aspects of EFI support and his strong expertise helps bring new exciting features to Sealbox Windows system encryption.
